# Qwen3.8-27B training artifact inventory

Snapshot: repository commit `3b64f462`, 2026-09-14. This is an offline evidence
index, not live state or launch authority. It covers every receipt-backed
Qwen3.8 training producer, checkpoint consumer, and current launch candidate in
the repository. Superseded preparation-only revisions are grouped with the run
lineage they prepared. No prompt, trace, reward, answer, flag, private log, live
API, Kubernetes, W&B, SFS, or external-benchmark content was read.

Evidence words are strict: **accepted** means a retained digest-valid receipt
explicitly accepts that exact gate; **prepared** means no run happened;
**infrastructure-invalid** means no scientific outcome was produced. A
successful controller alone is not training acceptance. “Comparable” below
means the named arms differ only in an intended study variable; it does not mean
that they improved task success.

## Contradictions and duplicates to keep visible

1. **No accepted Qwen3.8 RL checkpoint exists.** Parser, data, conversion, and
   engine diagnostics are not rewards or optimizer updates.
2. **No Qwen3.8 trained artifact is serving-qualified in the retained receipts.**
   Synthetic one-GPU loading is not registration or live base/candidate parity.
3. The old production-layout LR `1e-5` canary uses `manifest-outcome-v2`; the
   current LR1/LR30/LR100 arms use exact
   `manifest-available-a-outcome-v2`. Equal-looking counts cannot erase the
   provenance difference. No receipt here proves a complete 76-step current
   LR `1e-5` cell.
4. Current LR1, LR30, and LR100 share the exact current corpus and recipe, but
   only their common first six steps are presently comparable. LR30/LR100
   continuations must finish before an outcome comparison.
5. Dense teacher V5 and historical self-SFT are both valid training artifacts,
   but they are not a teacher-vs-self ablation: they differ in tasks, token dose,
   window count, epoch count, and source interface evidence.
6. W&B status is **unknown**, not failed, for dense teacher V5 and historical
   self-SFT because the retained sanitized terminal/export receipts do not bind
   it. Conversely, a W&B URL does not make an operational fixture a scientific
   result.
7. `docs/QWEN38_MILES_PRODUCTION_ARM.md` and the prod templates retain obsolete
   Dev5/Dev7 promotion wording. The current legal canary is prepared Miles
   Dev8; no production compilation may borrow earlier identities or acceptance.
8. WebExploitBench is absent from every recommendation here. It remains sealed
   until Fleet-dev selection is frozen and cannot choose an LR, checkpoint,
   stopping point, data mix, or retry.

## Next highest-value offline-preparable experiment

After completing the current LR30/LR100 arms and the Miles Dev8 canary, prepare
the **split-A teacher sampling pair** already locked by
[`qwen-blackbox-teacher-exposure-matched-control-v1.json`](../../../configs/studies/qwen-blackbox-teacher-exposure-matched-control-v1.json):

- treatment 1: family-balanced teacher sampling;
- treatment 2: availability-shaped, exposure-matched sampling;
- exact equal dose per arm: 50 episodes, 401 windows, 419,165 supervised
  tokens, or 51 nominal batches at global batch 8;
- hold model initialization, selected LR, batch, epoch, seed, tokenizer, chat
  template, dense target policy, training layout, and Fleet-dev protocol fixed;
- rank only by paired Fleet-dev full-task success after both exact checkpoints
  pass reload/export/serving parity.

This is higher value than adding another arbitrary LR, batch, or epoch arm: it
isolates **where demonstrations are sampled from** while matching episode,
window, and supervised-token exposure. It directly tests whether family balance
helps task-solving generalization instead of merely changing training dose.
Only an inert paired launch template and validation tests are legal offline;
materialization must remain blocked until LR30/LR100 Fleet-dev selection is
frozen. Split B is a later confirmation, not a parallel tuning surface. The
existing lock is metadata only and authorizes no job. [Design evidence](../../QWEN_TEACHER_EXPOSURE_MATCHED_CONTROL_2026-09-11.md)
